# Celoura Fashion – Custom Dress Ordering System

**Celoura Fashion** is a custom fashion and dress-ordering platform designed to help customers explore fashion designs and request tailor-made outfits according to their preferred design, colour, style, and requirements.

The system aims to improve the customer ordering experience, simplify order management, and provide an organized way to manage custom dress requests.

## 1. Project Overview

Celoura Fashion specializes in custom-made Lehenga designs and women's fashion. The proposed online system will provide a convenient platform for customers to discover designs, submit customization requirements, and keep track of their orders.

**Tagline:** *Your Dream Outfit, Designed Your Way.*

## 2. Project Objectives

- Provide an online platform to showcase custom dress designs.
- Allow customers to explore available designs and fashion collections.
- Enable customers to submit personalized dress-order requests.
- Support customization based on design, colour, style, and other preferences.
- Help the business manage customer details and dress orders efficiently.
- Improve communication between customers and the business.
- Provide a user-friendly and responsive interface across devices.

## 3. Project Scope

The system focuses on the online presentation of fashion designs and the management of custom dress orders.

### In Scope

- Fashion design gallery and catalogue.
- Customer registration and login.
- Custom dress-order submission.
- Design, colour, and style selection.
- Customer contact and order information management.
- Order status tracking.
- Administrative order management.
- Responsive user interface.
- Business information and contact details.
- Advanced AI-based fashion recommendations (AI Stylist, occasion & body-shape matching, bespoke tailoring blueprints).

### Out of Scope for the Initial Version

- Online payment gateway integration.
- Automated delivery and courier tracking.
- Full-scale inventory and supplier management.

These features may be considered for future development.

## 4. User Roles

### 4.1 Customer

Customers can:

- Browse available Lehenga designs and other women's fashion collections.
- View design images and relevant descriptions.
- Register and log in to their accounts.
- Submit custom dress-order requests.
- Specify preferred designs, colours, and styles.
- Provide measurements and additional requirements when requested.
- View their order details and status.
- Contact Celoura Fashion for further information.

### 4.2 Administrator

The administrator can:

- Manage customer information.
- Add, update, and remove fashion designs.
- Upload and manage design images.
- View and manage customer orders.
- Update order statuses.
- Review customization requirements.
- Maintain business contact information.
- Monitor and organize customer requests.

## 6. Non-Functional Requirements

- **Usability:** The system should be simple and easy to navigate.
- **Performance:** Pages and design images should load efficiently.
- **Security:** User credentials and customer information must be protected.
- **Reliability:** The system should handle customer requests and order records consistently.
- **Responsiveness:** The interface should work on desktops, tablets, and mobile devices.
- **Maintainability:** The application should be structured for easier updates and improvements.
- **Scalability:** The system should support future features and a growing number of customers.
- **Data Integrity:** Customer and order information should remain accurate and consistent.

## 7. Customer Order Workflow

1. The customer visits the Celoura Fashion website.
2. The customer browses the available fashion designs.
3. The customer selects a preferred design or requests a customized outfit.
4. The customer specifies the desired design, colour, style, measurements, and other relevant requirements.
5. The customer submits the order request with the necessary contact details.
6. The administrator reviews the request and discusses any additional requirements with the customer.
7. The order is updated as it progresses through the business workflow.
8. The customer can check the order status or contact the business for updates.

**Note:** Final pricing, measurements, fitting arrangements, and completion dates can be confirmed directly with the business.

## 8. Suggested Order Statuses

The following statuses can be used to manage the ordering process:

- **Pending:** The order request has been submitted.
- **Confirmed:** The order requirements have been reviewed and accepted.
- **In Progress:** The dress is being prepared or tailored.
- **Ready:** The dress is ready for the agreed collection or handover.
- **Completed:** The order has been completed.
- **Cancelled:** The order has been cancelled.
